Practice Areas

Areas of Legal Practice

Our practice covers a range of litigation and legal matters before courts and specialised tribunals.

01

Criminal Law

Our criminal law practice encompasses criminal litigation and proceedings before the High Court and District Courts, including matters arising at different stages of the criminal process. We undertake meticulous examination of the factual matrix, statutory provisions, evidentiary aspects and applicable procedural law, followed by appropriate legal strategy and preparation. Our representation is founded upon precise legal drafting, cogent legal submissions and focused advocacy, with due regard to the rights and interests of the client throughout the proceedings.

02

SARFAESI Matters

Our SARFAESI practice focuses on legal proceedings arising under the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002. We undertake careful examination of the underlying financial transaction, security documents, statutory notices and procedural compliance, and advise on appropriate legal remedies available under the applicable law. Representation is undertaken before the competent courts and tribunals with emphasis on precise pleadings, effective legal submissions and diligent conduct of proceedings.

03

Banking & Financial Laws

Our banking and financial law practice encompasses legal matters arising from banking transactions, financial arrangements, lending and recovery proceedings, and related disputes. We undertake careful examination of the contractual, statutory and regulatory aspects of each matter, including relevant financial documents and applicable legal provisions. Our approach emphasises appropriate legal remedies, precise drafting, effective legal submissions and diligent representation before the competent court or forum.

04

Property Disputes

Our property dispute practice encompasses legal matters relating to ownership, title, possession, transfer and other disputes concerning immovable property. We undertake detailed examination of title documents, relevant records, contractual arrangements and applicable statutory provisions to assess the legal issues involved and available remedies. The matter is approached through careful legal research, precise drafting, appropriate procedural strategy and focused representation before the competent court or forum.

05

Family Court Matters

Our Family Court practice encompasses legal proceedings arising from matrimonial and family disputes, including matters concerning marital rights, matrimonial reliefs, maintenance, custody and other allied family-law proceedings. Each matter is examined with due regard to the factual circumstances, applicable statutory provisions and procedural requirements. We focus on appropriate legal remedies, careful drafting, effective legal submissions and sensitive yet diligent representation before the competent court.

06

Civil Matters

Our civil litigation matters encompasses a broad range of civil proceedings involving contractual, property, recovery, injunction and other civil disputes. We undertake careful examination of the pleadings, documentary evidence, contractual arrangements, applicable statutory provisions and relevant judicial precedents. Each matter is approached with appropriate procedural strategy, precise drafting, cogent legal submissions and focused representation before the competent court or forum.

07

Service Matters

Our service law practice encompasses legal matters arising from public employment and service-related disputes before the competent courts and tribunals. We undertake careful examination of service records, applicable service rules, statutory provisions, administrative decisions and relevant judicial precedents. Each matter is approached through appropriate legal remedies, precise drafting, thorough procedural preparation and focused representation, with due regard to the legal rights and interests of the client.

Our Process

A Clear Approach to Every Matter

We believe that effective legal representation begins with understanding the matter clearly.

1

Consultation

We begin with a comprehensive consultation to understand the client's concerns, factual background and legal objectives. The matter is carefully examined to identify the relevant legal issues, possible remedies and the appropriate course of legal action.

2

Case Assessment

We undertake a detailed assessment of the facts, documents and applicable legal provisions relevant to the matter. This process helps identify the legal issues involved, evaluate the available remedies and determine the most appropriate course of legal proceedings.

3

Legal Strategy

Based on the facts, applicable statutes, judicial precedents and the circumstances of the matter, we formulate a clear and tailored legal strategy. Our approach focuses on appropriate legal remedies, procedural requirements and effective presentation of the client's case before the competent court or forum.

4

Advocacy & Representation

We undertake professional representation before the appropriate courts, tribunals and legal forums, supported by thorough preparation, sound legal submissions and focused advocacy. Our approach combines procedural diligence, legal analysis and effective presentation of the client's case at every stage of the proceedings.
