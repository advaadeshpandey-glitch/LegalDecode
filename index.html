Practice Areas

Areas of Legal Practice

Our practice covers a range of litigation and legal matters before courts and specialised tribunals.

01

Criminal Law

Our criminal law practice encompasses criminal litigation and proceedings before the High Court and District Courts, including matters arising at different stages of the criminal process. We undertake careful examination of the factual matrix, statutory provisions, evidentiary aspects and applicable procedural law, followed by appropriate legal strategy and preparation.

02

SARFAESI Matters

Our SARFAESI practice focuses on legal proceedings arising under the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002. We undertake careful examination of financial transactions, security documents, statutory notices and procedural compliance, and advise on appropriate legal remedies.

03

Banking & Financial Laws

Our banking and financial law practice encompasses legal matters arising from banking transactions, financial arrangements, lending and recovery proceedings and related disputes. Each matter is examined through its contractual, statutory and regulatory dimensions.

04

Property Disputes

Our property dispute practice encompasses legal matters relating to ownership, title, possession, transfer and other disputes concerning immovable property. We examine title documents, relevant records, contractual arrangements and applicable statutory provisions to assess available remedies.

05

Family Court Matters

Our Family Court practice encompasses legal proceedings arising from matrimonial and family disputes, including matters concerning marital rights, matrimonial reliefs, maintenance, custody and other allied family-law proceedings.

06

Civil Matters

Our civil litigation practice encompasses a broad range of civil proceedings involving contractual, property, recovery, injunction and other civil disputes. We undertake careful examination of pleadings, documentary evidence, contractual arrangements, statutory provisions and relevant judicial precedents.

07

Service Matters

Our service law practice encompasses legal matters arising from public employment and service-related disputes before competent courts and tribunals. We examine service records, applicable service rules, statutory provisions, administrative decisions and relevant judicial precedents.
