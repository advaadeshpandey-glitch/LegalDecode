About LegalDecode

A contemporary legal practice built around clarity and preparation.

LegalDecode is a Lucknow-based legal practice providing assistance in litigation, legal advisory work and proceedings before courts and tribunals. The work begins with understanding the factual background, identifying the legal questions that arise, examining the available record and considering the procedural position of the matter.

Our objective is to provide practical and carefully considered legal assistance while keeping the client's legitimate interests, procedural requirements and access to justice at the centre of our work.

Legal concerns can involve important personal, financial, commercial and professional consequences. We therefore seek to explain relevant legal issues in clear terms, identify documents and information that may be required, and discuss lawful options after considering the individual circumstances of the matter.

LegalDecode also recognises the importance of accessible legal information. Through responsible legal communication, we aim to make legal concepts easier to understand while maintaining the distinction between general information and advice based on a particular client's facts and documents.

Practical Legal Assistance Assistance shaped by the factual background, available documents, applicable law and procedural stage of the matter.
Careful Preparation Legal research, document review, drafting and procedural preparation form an important part of our working approach.
Client-Centred Communication We seek to communicate the relevant legal position and procedural considerations in a clear and understandable manner.
Professional Responsibility Each matter is considered according to its own facts, evidence, applicable law and professional obligations.
Access to Justice We value responsible legal awareness and the broader objective of making legal information more accessible to people who need it.
What we handle

Practice Areas

Legal assistance across the firm's core practice areas. The nature of assistance and available remedies depends on the facts, applicable law, forum jurisdiction and procedural position of each matter.

01 / PRACTICE AREA

Criminal Law

Assistance in criminal matters involving complaints, FIR-related issues, investigation, bail, criminal proceedings, trial matters and other remedies available under the applicable criminal law framework. The appropriate course depends on the allegations, evidence, procedural stage and jurisdiction of the relevant court.

02 / PRACTICE AREA

SARFAESI Matters

Assistance in matters arising under the SARFAESI framework, including statutory notices, enforcement measures, possession-related disputes and proceedings before the competent forum. Relevant notices, dates, loan documents and the stage of enforcement may be important to determining the available legal options.

03 / PRACTICE AREA

Banking & Financial Laws

Legal assistance in banking and financial disputes, recovery-related proceedings, disputes concerning financial transactions and associated statutory remedies. The legal position is considered with reference to the transaction documents, applicable legislation and jurisdiction of the forum concerned.

04 / PRACTICE AREA

Property Disputes

Assistance in property-related disputes concerning title, possession, agreements, partition, injunctions, specific relief and connected civil remedies. The factual history, documentary record, limitation and jurisdictional requirements may be relevant to the assessment of a property dispute.

05 / PRACTICE AREA

Family Court Matters

Assistance in family-related proceedings, including matrimonial disputes, maintenance, custody and other matters falling within the jurisdiction of the appropriate family court or other competent forum. Each matter requires consideration of its individual circumstances and the applicable personal and procedural laws.

06 / PRACTICE AREA

Civil Matters

Assistance in civil suits, contractual disputes, injunctions, recovery-related claims, interim relief and other civil proceedings. The cause of action, pleadings, supporting documents, limitation, jurisdiction and relief sought are relevant considerations in assessing the appropriate legal course.

07 / PRACTICE AREA

Service Matters

Assistance in service-related disputes concerning employment conditions, departmental proceedings, promotion, service benefits and other employment-related issues. The applicable service rules, relevant orders, dates and jurisdiction of the competent forum are considered according to the circumstances of the matter.

At LegalDecode, we take the responsibilities entrusted to us seriously. We work to understand each matter, identify relevant legal issues and consider appropriate lawful remedies with integrity, careful preparation and focused advocacy.

Courts & forums

Representation before relevant forums

Forum selection and representation depend on the subject matter, territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction, applicable legislation and procedural requirements.

High Court

Matters before the Lucknow Bench of the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad, subject to the nature and jurisdiction of the case.

District Courts

Civil, criminal, trial-level and other proceedings before the appropriate District Courts, according to the nature of the matter.

Tribunals

Matters before the Debt Recovery Tribunal, Central Administrative Tribunal, State Public Services Tribunal and Armed Forces Tribunal, where applicable.

Other Competent Forums

Appropriate statutory authorities and forums, where the relevant dispute falls within their jurisdiction.

Our approach

A considered process for every matter

A structured approach helps identify the relevant facts, legal issues and procedural requirements before deciding upon the next steps.

01 · Initial Consultation

Understand the client's concern, relevant background, immediate requirements and the present stage of the matter.

02 · Case Assessment

Examine available documents, relevant dates, jurisdiction, procedural history and the legal questions involved.

03 · Legal Preparation

Research the applicable law, consider available lawful remedies and prepare the necessary documents and submissions.

04 · Representation

Undertake appropriate procedural steps and representation before the competent court or forum, as agreed for the matter.
